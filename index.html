My Favourite Hobbies and Healthy Lifestyle

Staying Active and Balanced

Living a healthy and balanced life is very important to me. Almost every morning, I start my day with a cup of black coffee before doing my fitness routine. I enjoy incline walking on the treadmill because it builds stamina and keeps my heart strong. Practising yoga is another activity that I truly love, as it improves my flexibility, posture, and calm focus.

Fun Activities and Nutritious Food

Outside of my daily workouts, I love playing pool as a woman who enjoys a calm, focused game. It is a relaxing cue sport that requires steady concentration, patience, and smart strategy. To support my active lifestyle, I prefer eating simple, whole foods such as boiled eggs, brown rice, grilled chicken, fresh fruits, and drinking plenty of plain water.

My Top Daily Habits

  • Morning black coffee and incline treadmill walking
  • Yoga sessions for flexibility and calm focus
  • Playing pool to practise patience and accuracy
  • Eating whole foods and drinking plain water every day
